Field notes

A triangle gives a load a path into the supports. Without bracing, a long deck has to do too much bending on its own.

Members can pull or push; a long, slender piece under compression is more likely to buckle. Without beams connected to the lower joints, the deck has an 8 kg hanging-load limit.
